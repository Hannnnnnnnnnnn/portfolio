About Han Kim

I'm a designer working across brand identity, game interfaces, and generative AI. My background runs from concept art and packaging through graphic design and into product and web — which means I've had to judge quality in a lot of different languages, not just the one I'm most fluent in.

​

What I've come to care about most is the gap between work that looks right and work that holds up in use. A layout can be beautiful and still fail at arm's length. An AI-generated image can be technically flawless and still read as generic. Most of my work is spent finding where that line sits and explaining why.

​

Currently designing and building Shopify storefronts at ATACZ in Vancouver, BC — UX research, Figma, and front-end implementation. That work lives in a separate case-study site, written up in more depth than a gallery allows.
