About Han Kim

I’m a product designer at Atacz, a small upcycled bag brand where I own the product experience end to end — research, interface, front-end, and the measurement afterwards. Five years in, working across disciplines is the part I’d keep.

​

I like problems where the answer isn’t more, but better placed. Most of my work last year was deciding what had earned its space on a page and removing the rest — then finding out whether that was right.

​

Working somewhere this small means the same person notices a problem, argues for fixing it, designs the fix, builds it, and finds out whether it worked. That has shaped how I design more than any single project has. Because I build the front-end for what I design, I think in components, states, and edge cases from the first sketch rather than discovering them at handoff. And I’ve learned to be careful about what a result proves — one of my metrics last year pointed three different directions depending on the window I chose, and the useful thing was saying so.

​

Before Atacz I was a graphic designer at KITS Eyecare and Vancouver Premium Packaging, and a UX/UI designer on game titles at Sprung Studios — brand, packaging, game interfaces, and generative AI. That work is under Visual, and it’s why I judge quality in more than one language.
