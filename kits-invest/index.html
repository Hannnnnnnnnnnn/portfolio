Brochure Design

Integrated Stakeholder Event Identity: 2024 KITS Investor Day

Role: Graphic Designer

Deliverables: Event Logo / Presentation Deck / Large Format Signage / Attendee Credentials
Tools: Photoshop, Illustrator, Keynote

Project Overview

Designed the comprehensive visual experience for KITS’ most critical annual stakeholder event. The challenge was to establish a commanding, professional identity that inspired confidence among high-level investors, while simultaneously translating dense financial strategies into clear, digestible visual presentations. I managed the entire design lifecycle, from initial concept to final print production, ensuring absolute brand consistency across all physical and digital touchpoints.

Event Logo Identity

Establishing the Event Visual Identity Designed to evoke confidence, professionalism, and forward momentum for a high-stakes investor audience. This logo served as the foundational anchor for all subsequent event materials, ensuring a unified and recognizable brand presence across digital and physical platforms.

Poster Design - Standalone Graphic

Distilling Key Messages into Impactful Graphics Created a standalone poster designed to command attention in a busy environment. Prioritized strong typographical hierarchy and clean composition to ensure the event's key theme and information were immediately understood at a glance, from a distance.

Presentation Deck

Structuring Complex Data for Clarity Transformed over 50 pages of raw financial data and strategic planning into a visually engaging and highly legible presentation deck. Utilized strict typographic hierarchy and clean data visualization techniques to ensure complex information was immediately understandable to the audience during live presentations.

Lanyard & Badge Credentials

Attendee Experience & Brand Details Extended the event branding to tangible touchpoints. The badge design prioritized information hierarchy for quick attendee identification, while the lanyard involved detailed spec management for fabric printing, ensuring brand integrity down to the smallest detail.

Large Format Signage

High-Impact Environmental Graphics Designed large-format posters to establish a strong brand presence and aid navigation within the event venue. Managed the end-to-end print production process, coordinating with vendors to ensure precise color reproduction and optimal resolution for large-scale viewing.
