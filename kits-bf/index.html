KITS Black Friday 2025 Campaign

Campaign visuals for KITS’ record Black Friday week

Role
Graphic Designer
Deliverables
Website banners, Emails, Ads
Tools
Photoshop, Illustrator, After Effects, Nano Banana, Topaz, Veo3.1

Project Overview

I designed the visuals for KITS’ Black Friday and Cyber Monday campaign: website banners, emails and ads, built on one high-contrast neon motion concept. It ran during the company’s best week on record. The sales figures below are KITS’ results for that week, not a measure of the design on its own.

Design Strategy

Concept: cyberpunk motion. Instead of static imagery, the assets moved: neon purple type and floating product layers, so the same idea held on social and on the site.

Efficiency and scale. Marketing spend was held at about 14.3% of revenue, so each asset had to stretch. I built the campaign as a modular set, so banners, emails and ads stayed consistent across every size and placement.

Record week. $5.8 million in revenue over Black Friday and Cyber Monday week, +22% YoY.

Glasses. 16,800 units and $1.2 million in glasses revenue, units +35% YoY.

November. Glasses revenue for the month grew 56% YoY.

Run rate. KITS passed a $300 million revenue run rate for the first time.

“This was a breakthrough week for KITS and for our mission to build a leading consumer eyecare brand in North America,”

Roger Hardy, Co-Founder and CEO of KITS.

Email design: “The Trigger”

Mobile-first newsletters. Built for clarity and urgency. The neon motif carried into the inbox, so an email and the page it opened looked like one campaign. Email is how KITS reaches repeat customers, who brought in 65% of revenue.

PLP banners: “The Sustainer”

Between the product grids. These banners broke up long category pages without stopping the browse. I adapted the heavy neon graphics into horizontal layouts that point to one category each.
