Graphic Design

KITS Black Friday 2025 Campaign

Driving $5.8M Record Revenue Through Strategic Design

Role: Graphic Designer

Deliverables: Website banners, Emails, Ads
Tools: Photoshop, Illustrator, After Effect, Nano Banana, Topaz, Veo3.1

Project Overview

Led the visual direction for KITS' most successful week in history. This integrated campaign utilized a high-contrast 'Cyberpunk' concept to cut through the noise, directly contributing to a record-breaking $5.8 million in revenue over Black Friday & Cyber Monday week. The design strategy focused on high-impact motion graphics and scalability, supporting a volume of over 16,900 glasses orders without compromising brand integrity.

Design Strategy

Concept: Cyberpunk Motion To capture the attention of digital-native consumers, I moved away from static imagery to dynamic motion graphics featuring neon purple typography and floating product layers. This approach maximized engagement across social and web platforms.

Efficiency & Scale With marketing spend optimized at ~14.3% of revenue, the creative assets needed to be highly efficient. I built a modular design system that maintained visual consistency across thousands of touchpoints, driving a 65% revenue contribution from repeat customers.

Historic Revenue: Generated $5.8 Million in a single week (+35% YoY).

Record Volume: Over 16,900 pairs of glasses ordered (+47% YoY).

Category Growth: Glasses revenue in November grew by 56% YoY, proving the strong adoption of the campaign visuals.

Business Impact: Surpassed a $300 million run rate for the first time, demonstrating the ability to scale volume effectively.

“This was a breakthrough week for KITS and for our mission to build a leading consumer eyecare brand in North America,”

Roger Hardy, Co-Founder and CEO of KITS.

Email Design: "The Trigger”

Direct-to-Consumer Engagement (Email) Designed mobile-first newsletters focusing on clarity and urgency. By carrying the neon motif into the inbox, we ensured a seamless transition from email to the website, contributing significantly to the high repeat customer revenue (65%).

PLP Banners: "The Sustainer”

Seamless Merchandising (PLP Banners) Placed between product grids, these banners acted as visual breakers that maintained the shopping momentum. I adapted the heavy cyberpunk graphics into horizontal layouts to highlight specific categories without overwhelming the browsing experience.

Transformed static assets into dynamic video using Veo 3.1. Focused on natural head movements and blinking to reduce the "uncanny valley" effect.
